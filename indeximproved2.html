About

Digital AI Evidence Verifier helps people examine digital media and identify possible AI-generated or manipulated content. It inspects a file for patterns that tend to differ between camera recordings and synthetic media, then reports a classification, a confidence score and the signals behind it.

Results are estimates. A confident result is a lead for further review and does not prove where a file came from.

Media
Images (JPG, PNG, WebP) and videos (MP4, MOV, WebM)
Checks
AI tool markers, camera data, image size, pixel noise
Output
Classification, confidence, analysis signals, file details
Build status
